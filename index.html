About TaponTama

TaponTama looks at a photo of one piece of trash and tells you which segregation bin it belongs in: Biodegradable, Recyclable or Residual.

It is a student learning project (ELECT IT-E1 Applied Machine Learning, PUP Santa Rosa Campus). It can be wrong. When in doubt, follow the signs on the bins and the campus staff.

Limitations

  • One item per photo. Several items at once will confuse it.
  • It was trained on our own photos of common campus items, so unusual items may be misread.
  • Special waste (batteries, medicine, electronics, broken glass) is not covered. Ask the campus staff where it goes.
  • If it is less than 70% sure, it says Not sure instead of guessing.

Privacy: your photo is checked on your own phone. It is never uploaded or saved.
